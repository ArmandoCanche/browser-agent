# DeepBrowse AI

**Multi-Level Web Intelligence Agent**

---

### What I can do

Send me a URL and I'll dive deep — extracting content, navigating sub-pages automatically, and synthesizing everything into a clear answer.

---

### How to use it

Paste a URL on the first line, then your question on the second:

```
https://www.koenig-solutions.com/
What Microsoft Azure courses are available?
```

Or just paste a URL for a general summary — no question needed.

---

### What to expect

- **Level 1** — I extract and summarize the main page
- **Level 2** — I follow the most relevant links automatically
- **Level 3** — I go deeper on demand
- **Compare mode** — Ask me to compare two sites side by side

---

> _"Give me a URL and a question. I'll do the rest."_
